🛡️ Immediate Survival Guidelines

  • Flood: Move upward immediately; avoid moving water over 6 inches deep.
  • Earthquake: Drop, Cover, Hold. Stay clear of glass facades and overhead lines.
  • Cyclone: Secure storm shutters, stay indoors, keep mobile phones charged.
  • Wildfire: Close air vents, pack critical IDs, evacuate in upwind corridors.
  • Heatwave: Hydrate continuously; avoid strenuous sun exposure between 12-4 PM.
